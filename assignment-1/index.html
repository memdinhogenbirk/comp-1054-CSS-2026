Overview

About Metric

Metric is a Canadian rock band who is known for mixing synth and traditional rock adjacent instruments. They were originally formed in 1998 under the name "Mainstream", and changed their name to Metric after the release of their first album. The band consists of 4 members, Emily Haines, James Shaw, Joshua Winstead, and Joules Scott-Key. As of 2023, the band has released 9 studio albums, and a total of 283 songs (as per Genius.com).

The Meaning of the Lyrics

The song "Now or Never Now" was released ahead of its album "Art of Doubt", on September 4th, 2018. The lyrics of the song are meant to illustrate the feeling of being at a metaphorical crossroads in life. They relay the feelings of someone who is at a low point, struggling to pick themself up. The title "Now or Never Now" is a twist on the common phrase "Now or never", with the added "now" being used to stress that NOW is the time to act, or be forever stuck in the mire.
